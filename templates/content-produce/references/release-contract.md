# Content release contract

Use the actual destination and the reviewed edition/revision. A prepared or reviewed edition is not a delivery.

1. Resolve the applicable exact or standing authority: destination/account, version or selection policy, timing and permitted action. Reuse existing permission; a draft request alone supplies no send authority.
2. Verify the final edition and packaging in the selected tool. Keep canonical material and independent channel editions linked.
3. Deliver only the selected action. Editing, review and outward delivery remain distinct even when one skill or an authorized automation carries them through.
4. Record actual per-destination state and output identity: ready, scheduled, published/sent, failed or outcome unknown. One successful destination is not rolled back by another's failure; keep Partially published plus exact per-destination evidence until unresolved deliveries are reconciled.
5. For an unknown outcome, inspect the service's actual send/post/job identity before retrying. Never create a second send merely because a response timed out.
6. Report real output links/IDs and unresolved input. Do not infer enquiries, attribution or sales from a successful publication.

Daily upkeep may reconcile supported delivery facts and owner-stated decisions under its selected policy. Editorial learning chooses experiments and proposes strategic/taste changes; it does not silently redefine goals. An explicit owner correction may be curated at its actual Knowledge owner.
