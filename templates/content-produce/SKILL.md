# Content produce and distribute

Before creating a record or changing Status/flags, apply the configured key convention and integration triggers/safe states; inspect them first if unrecorded. Choose an edition key before create and query that retained key before retrying an uncertain outcome.

Template edition 2026-11-05.1. Use the owner’s actual Content configuration and routes. Read [adoption](references/adaptation.md) when tailoring this into a personal skill; normal use loads only the selected configuration and relevant material. This template is not an installed or enabled method.

Accept actual text, audio or footage with its original location/revision, chosen primary goal/revision, any secondary goal, audience, relevant voice/taste and delivery scope. Material may come from Prepare or an existing human workflow. Do not require the owner to recreate it through our earlier stages.

Finish the original in the chosen tool. Notion is the reference editorial home; a video project owns its actual timeline/media edits. HyperFrames is one candidate. Use it only after practical qualification in this environment and availability of an actually reviewed operating skill; neither is established by this template. Preserve the chosen creative defaults. Keep a suitable existing editor or human editor when selected.

Create only editions that have a useful purpose for their destination. A newsletter may be an original or a synthesis; a short text need not begin as a video. Adapt context, hook, length, image and next step to the actual channel/account constraints. Never claim a video extract exists without the real footage/time range.

Keep an independently edited edition linked to its original/sources. Preserve one canonical body for shared text and separate actual deliveries. A library entry, Resources lesson, web article and email can reuse material without becoming competing editable originals.

Review the actual final version for meaning, factual support, taste/voice, packaging and audience fit. Inspect exported video by seeing and hearing it, including audio sync, readable text, captions and ending. A screenshot or valid container is not video acceptance.

The editing handoff is a reviewed edition ready for its destination. This does not mean it has been sent or published.

## Distribution

Read [the release contract](references/release-contract.md) when sending, scheduling or publishing. Prepare Kit subject/preview/body and relevant public companions when selected. Send, schedule or publish only under the applicable exact or standing destination/version authority. A standing automated policy need not ask again for every routine release; a draft request supplies no send authority.

Record the actual state and verified result per destination, including unknown or partial outcomes as defined by the selected configuration and setup contract. Reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying to prevent duplicate sends/posts. A failed email does not erase a successful web delivery. Return real output links/IDs and remaining input; do not invent publication or marketing results.
