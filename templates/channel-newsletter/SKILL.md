## Procedure

1. Read the sources above.
2. Draft the broad piece for this period. Cover the strands the themes allow. This Edition is the parent a derivative links to.
3. Run the [quality-gate skill](../channel-quality-gate/SKILL.md). The human sees the draft only after it passes.
4. Leave human approval unset. Do not send or schedule.

The newsletter stays broader than any post derived from it. Derivative angles are chosen by the [newsletter-to-posts skill](../channel-newsletter-to-posts/SKILL.md).
