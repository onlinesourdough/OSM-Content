## Procedure

1. Read the existing cadence and upcoming work. Reuse record identities, existing dates and actual delivery results. Resolve only choices needed for this plan; invent no days, volumes or posting times.
2. Propose upcoming work within the selected rhythm and capacity, applying category limits only where selected. Link existing drafts and identify gaps; do not create redundant productions to fill a calendar.
3. Keep proposed dates separate from live scheduled jobs. Send due work through its channel method and quality gate. In the owner–assistant review loop, the owner comments, the assistant revises and resolves addressed comments, then the owner reviews again. Recheck changed work before returning it; hold unresolved issues.
4. Only the owner approves the final version and destinations. Hold unapproved or changed pieces. An approved plan is not approval of every future draft.
5. Return a plan with links, proposed dates where chosen, review needs and next useful action. Create no automation, delivery job or publication. A separately authorized delivery method must verify approval, destination authority and the actual job/result before recording Scheduled or Published.

Optional best practice from the first real run: a short approval window for the next few days, with review ping-pong between owner and assistant. No minutes, named statuses or posting times are prescribed. A bank replenished on a chosen cycle and owner-named value categories with caps may help. Use only if chosen. Adopting this skill does not start a recurring caller.
