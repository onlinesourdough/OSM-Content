# Newsletter to posts · optional helper

Template generation 2026-11-24.1. This helper coordinates selected channel methods; it is not a mandatory stage or another channel. Read [adoption](../content-channel/references/adaptation.md).

## Inputs

The selected newsletter revision, owner-selected destinations, existing channel strategy and reuse policy, relevant source permissions, optional cadence and the target channel skills. Resolve their actual references before use. Keep business context at its existing owner.

## Procedure

1. Read the selected original and the owner's reuse policy. If a material choice is missing, hold only the dependent work rather than inventing a shared rule.
2. Inspect existing production records and drafts. Reuse identities and preserve the owner's chosen grouping. Link the source revision and the permitted material selected for reuse.
3. Hand each requested piece and its goal to that channel's working method. That method assembles the output and runs the quality gate; this helper does not duplicate its procedure.
4. Request owner review only after issues and comments are addressed and the gate passes. Keep version/destination approval separate. Do not schedule or publish.
