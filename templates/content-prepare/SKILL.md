# Content prepare

Before creating a record or changing Status/flags, apply the configured key convention and integration triggers/safe states; inspect them first if unrecorded. Choose an edition key before create and query that retained key before retrying an uncertain outcome.

Template edition 2026-11-05.1. Use the owner’s actual Content configuration and routes. Read [adoption](references/adaptation.md) when tailoring this into a personal skill; normal use loads only the selected configuration and relevant material. This template is not an installed or enabled method.

Start from the chosen idea or actual work and the primary goal and its revision, any selected secondary goal, audience, evidence, voice/taste and working preferences. Reuse decisions already present. Determine the specific useful promise and what the creator can honestly demonstrate or explain.

Choose an appropriate starting medium and form: article, newsletter, written guide, how-to demonstration, showcase, interview or direct short piece. Do not invent a minimum length or make someone film a lifestyle they do not want to share.

Prepare what this creator actually needs. A writer may need an outline and successive drafts; another person wants a full spoken script, a shot/screen-capture list, examples, visual cues or only short notes. Preserve an agreed full-script requirement. Keep the work in its selected canonical editorial home.

Plan reusable source material without breaking the original story: understandable sections, real examples, readable screen capture and usable audio where relevant. Proposed excerpts are ideas until actual text/footage and, for video, real time ranges exist.

Support the creator during writing/recording where requested. Be clear about the handoff: a prepared plan is not a filmed video. Source material is ready only when the actual writing, recording or other original exists, is accessible and has a known location/revision. Prepare owns developing that original until the selected workflow hands it to Produce for finishing. This is a clear material handoff, not an extra mandatory human approval: reuse the chosen manual, assisted or automated decision policy.

Preserve attribution, uncertainty, client privacy and human originals. Do not replace a chosen analog process with AI drafting by default. Missing essential evidence or footage remains explicit while independent preparation proceeds.

Return the concrete brief/draft/recording package, the actual remaining human input and the next handoff. Link real source material when available. No editor installation, rendering, sending or external publication is included merely by preparing the piece.
