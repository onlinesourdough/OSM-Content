# Content intake

Template edition 2026-11-05.1. Use the owner’s actual Content configuration and routes. Read [adoption](references/adaptation.md) when tailoring this into a personal skill; normal use loads only the selected configuration and relevant material. This template is not an installed or enabled method.

Load the selected Content route and only the relevant current goals/audience context. Use the owner's actual schema. Before writing, verify row-level integration triggers and a non-publishing draft state as well as property types. Choose or reuse a stable key before create, retain it through uncertainty and query it before retry; do not generate another key on a lost response. A one-off safe capture can use inspected mappings without forcing a saved configuration or full setup. Capture can be a conversation: accept a rough braindump and useful follow-up corrections without forcing a form.

Before creating a record, reconcile stable source ID/URL and semantic overlap, including parked material. Preserve the original words and attribution; distinguish editorial notes and inferred angles. Unknown source dates and unread links remain explicit. A correction updates the existing idea instead of creating another copy.

Store why the owner saved it, the real source/location, relevant context and one concrete next action when needed. Treat third-party inspiration, the owner's raw ideas and explicitly approved voice examples differently; do not infer voice approval from an AI draft or public performance.

Propose a few useful perspectives tied to the actual audience and selected goal. Include what the owner can substantiate or add. A source's popularity is not evidence that its claims apply to this business. Keep personal observations when they serve a real editorial purpose; every capture does not need a direct sales pitch.

When an idea is selected for production, link one primary goal and, when useful, a secondary goal with the relevant revisions. Capture alone does not require choosing every field. Suggest written, recorded, combined, or direct short form where it helps. Long form is not a mandatory prerequisite. Do not mark a proposed idea Selected unless the owner or the configured decision policy selected it.

Saving can be the whole task. Return the real record/readback link and a concise angle/next step; continue to Prepare only when that work is requested or already covered by the selected workflow.

For scheduled scouting, use only the configured sources, criteria and bounded discovery window. New accounts or broad feed access are not implied. No worthwhile idea is a valid result. Do not turn the scout into an automatic production or publishing job.
