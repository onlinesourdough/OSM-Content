# Channel format

Create one Knowledge page per active channel. Title it with the channel name. This page is the only owner of that channel's purpose, audience, language, skeleton, length, and visuals.

Signature and formatting rules live on the [red thread](red-thread.md). This page links to them and does not restate them. Voice lives on the [voice profile](voice-profile.md). The cap and any slot live on [cadence](cadence.md).

This form is the method source. The owner's filled page is the runtime source. The [setup interview](setup-interview.md) fills empty fields once. Role examples in [configuration](../../docs/configuration.md) are starting points; the chosen purpose is written here.

## Skeleton

The section order for this channel. Leave it blank until the owner sets it. A placeholder order to replace, not a required shape: opening, point, support, close.

## Length

The limit the owner uses for this channel, including the unit and how it is counted. Leave it blank until the owner sets it. Do not bake a platform default into a skill.

## Visuals

Required, optional, or none. When visuals are required, name the kind the owner uses. Media files stay in the mapped storage from [setup](../../docs/setup.md).
