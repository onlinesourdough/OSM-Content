# Cadence

One Knowledge page. It is the only calendar for content. Channel skills link to the row they need. They do not copy a cap or a slot.

This form is the method source. The owner's filled page is the runtime source. No personal cadence is included. Leave every row empty until the [setup interview](setup-interview.md) records the owner's answer. An explicit "no cap" is a complete cap. A blank cap is an unfinished interview.

A slot does not grant permission to schedule or publish. Approval stays on the [quality gate](quality-gate.md), and the status for a piece stays on the Edition.
