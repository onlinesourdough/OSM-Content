# Cadence · optional planning prompts

Use an existing Knowledge working-preference record or its selected equivalent. A cadence makes workload and upcoming review visible. These prompts add no mandatory page, calendar or rhythm. Link an existing scheduler/calendar rather than duplicating it.

## Choices to record only when selected

- Active channel and goal reference:
- Rhythm or on-demand use:
- Timezone, if timed slots are chosen:
- Planning horizon, optional:
- Capacity or caps, optional, with period and counting rule:
- Value categories and caps, optional; the owner names them:
- Owner review route and approval evidence: link existing policy.
- Work-bank replenishment/review cycle, optional:
- Actual planning/calendar route, and any separately authorized job identities:

A blank optional choice means unselected or unresolved, not an invented default. Only a choice needed for a timed or bounded plan holds that planning operation; it does not prevent an ordinary draft.

A proposed slot grants no permission to schedule or publish. Use the [schedule skeleton](../content-schedule/SKILL.md) to plan, the channel method to assemble a piece, and the [quality gate](quality-gate.md) before owner review. Only the owner marks the final version/destinations approved.
