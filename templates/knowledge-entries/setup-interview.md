# Setup interview

Ask this once. Read each destination first. A filled field stands; do not ask it again. Write the answer only onto its destination. Leave the answer out of this page, out of the owner configuration, and out of every skill.

This form is the method source. The owner's note that the interview is complete is a date plus links, stored on the owner configuration described in [configuration](../../docs/configuration.md). The facts themselves live on the Knowledge pages below.

After the answers are stored, measure the voice profile from the linked posts before the first draft. Ask the owner only when a measurement is ambiguous and the posts do not resolve it. Then draft with the channel skill and run the quality gate. The human sees the draft that passes.

## 1. Own posts to measure voice from

Ask, per active channel and language: which of your own posts should voice be measured from?

Destination: evidence links on the [voice profile](voice-profile.md). Do not ask the owner to describe their voice. Do not paste the posts into the profile or into a skill. Follow [writing voice](../../docs/writing-voice.md) for authorship and permission.

## 2. Per-channel language and format

Ask, per active channel, only for fields that are still empty: purpose, audience, language, skeleton, length, and visuals.

Destination: that channel's [format page](channel-format.md). Purpose and audience that already live on a business record are linked, not copied. Language is stored only here.

Open the interview by confirming the active channels against the owner configuration. Add a channel only when the owner chooses it. Role examples stay in [configuration](../../docs/configuration.md).

## 3. Red-thread themes

Ask: where is positioning already recorded, and which themes form the red thread?

Destination: a link to the existing positioning record, and the themes on the [red thread](red-thread.md). If no record owns positioning, the positioning statement is written once on the red thread. Do not copy a page that already owns it.

## 4. Format and signature rules

Ask: which signature and formatting rules should a reader recognize on every platform?

Destination: the signature section of the [red thread](red-thread.md). Confirm or replace the raw-formatting starter on that form. Channel skeletons stay on the format pages and are not copied here.

## 5. Private boundaries

Ask: which private details must never be named? Confirm or replace the starter list, home city and family details, and add any others.

Destination: the boundary list on the [red thread](red-thread.md), with the date it was confirmed. Do not store an example city, a family name, or any real private detail in the shared module. Skills read this list from the owner's red-thread page.

## 6. Inspirations and what is too raw

Ask: which inspirations may inform the work, and what is too raw to publish?

Destination: links to Source records on the [red thread](red-thread.md), and the rawness line on that same page. How a reference is used stays in the [reference-library workflow](../../docs/reference-library.md).

## 7. Cadence caps

Ask, per active channel: what is the cap for a period, and is there a slot you have already chosen?

Destination: the [cadence](cadence.md) page. An explicit "no cap" is an answer. A blank field is not. Leave the slot empty unless the owner names one. Do not invent a day, a time, or a weekly calendar.

## 8. Approval rules

Ask: who may approve publication, which Edition status counts as that approval, and should a second model review the draft before you see it?

Destination: the approval rule and the second-model switch on the [quality gate](quality-gate.md). The agent is not an approver. The status value for a given piece is stored later on that Edition, as defined in [setup](../../docs/setup.md).

## Close

Record `interview_completed` on the owner configuration with the date and the Knowledge page links. Do not paste the answers there. The first draft may start when every destination the gate reads is filled or explicitly declined.
