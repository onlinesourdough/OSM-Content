# From an idea to a useful post

Use this for a selected short idea or reference. Reuse the existing Content
records and owner configuration; a single post does not need new setup,
long-form production or another database.

## Choose what this edition is for

Keep a short brief beside the draft:

- Who is reading, and on which channel and in which language?
- What should they understand, feel able to do or want to explore?
- What is the author's own observation, perspective or demonstrated work?
- Which source supports the piece, and how will its creator be credited?

Use the actual channel goal. Customer dialogue, audience growth, education and
discovery of longer work need different writing and evidence. The same idea
can serve different purposes on two channels. Do not infer the goal from the
platform's name or impose the adopting owner's choices on everyone else.

## Write and choose an ending

Start with the actual point. Keep the author's useful wording and reasoning.
Use a relevant source to strengthen the explanation, then add the author's
own contribution. Do not turn another creator's experience into their anecdote.

Choose the ending from the purpose of this edition:

| Purpose | A useful ending |
| --- | --- |
| Reflection or discovery | Let the idea stand; no compulsory question or CTA |
| Learn more | Link a real relevant guide, example or longer video |
| Customer dialogue | Offer a specific next step when it follows naturally |
| Discussion | Ask a question the author actually wants answered |

A brand mention should explain the connection. An external link should help
the reader continue. Neither is required in every post. Do not claim that
adding or removing a link guarantees more reach.

## Review the actual version

Check meaning, evidence, authorship, voice, channel fit and actual length.
Keep approval separate from publication. For shared media, distinguish an
attributed quote/share from permission to upload another person's video.
Use the selected format and rights; attribution alone is not upload permission.

Store each independently edited text on its Edition, linked to the production
and Source. Revisions update that original. A repeat reference lookup must
reuse the Source identity. Publication needs its applicable destination and
version authority, then a verified result before it is marked delivered.

## Learn against the chosen purpose

Keep the observation date, time window and source with the edition. Reach,
profile interest, useful replies, traffic to a video and customer enquiries
are distinct signals. Missing analytics is unknown, not zero. An impression
does not prove trust, a lead or a sale, and an outbound click does not prove a
completed video view.

Keep one useful lesson or next experiment. Update a lasting preference at its
existing configuration or method owner. Accepted writing becomes a voice
example only when the author accepts it as representative.
