## Reuse the business foundation

Read the existing goals/vision, audience, offers, positioning, voice, channels and working preferences. Link their actual owning records and revisions. Resolve a missing business decision there rather than creating a competing Content strategy. A creator can pursue suitable enquiries, sales, education, a community role, audience growth or another goal; do not silently optimize every business for followers.

Select the goal per edition, not only per production. A single idea can build an audience on one channel and support customer dialogue on another. Record the reader, language, useful ending and relevant evidence for each. Reach, profile interest, video discovery and customer enquiries are different signals; none is a universal success measure. Monetization ideas remain provisional until the owner chooses them. Concrete observations stay with the Content record or edition, with date/window and uncertainty.

For an ordinary post, use the [short-post workflow](post-workflow.md). Do not restart Setup. A link, brand mention, question or sales invitation is optional and follows the purpose of that edition.

## Start from the existing business context

Keep the intended contribution of content clear: what the owner wants to build, who it should help and what would make it worthwhile. Reuse the business foundation instead of inventing a separate strategy store for the module.

| Context | Owning record to find or adapt |
| --- | --- |
| Direction and goals | Existing vision, priorities and goals; a compact Content vision can describe how content contributes to them |
| Audience and perspective | Existing audience, positioning, offers and the owner's point of view |
| Voice and visual expression | Existing writing/style guide, accepted examples and rejected treatments |
| Working choices | One personal Content configuration linking those records, selected channels, tools, media routes, methods and delivery authority |

These are responsibilities, not compulsory filenames or four new pages. Reuse suitable Knowledge records and link their actual owners. Split a record only when it has a distinct maintenance responsibility. Sources, drafts, editions and feedback remain in Content, not in a second vision document.

## Give each channel a useful role

Choose a small set that fits the owner and the audience. The examples below are starting points, not rules about how everybody must use a platform.

| Channel | Possible role | Useful content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | Explain and demonstrate work in depth, with material people can return to | Tutorials, practical examples, walkthroughs and selected shorter videos |
| Newsletter / email list | Build a continuing relationship with subscribers through direct, deliberately selected editions | Written reflections, useful lessons, curated examples and replies |
| LinkedIn | Share professional expertise and start relevant conversations in the chosen market | Written breakdowns, work examples, lessons and appropriate invitations |
| X / Twitter | Share concise observations, join conversations and build a recognizable personal perspective | Short ideas, work in progress, useful replies and links to deeper work when relevant |
| Instagram | An optional visual or personal expression when it serves a chosen purpose | Visual examples, behind-the-scenes material or a selected personal angle |

For each active channel, record its purpose, reader, language, useful formats, ending and evidence of progress. A local-language customer channel and an English personal-brand channel can have different jobs. Choose the actual market; a creator's use of LinkedIn is not a universal country-specific rule.

Newsletter is a channel in its own right, not merely a place to send recycled posts. A useful idea may appear in several places, but each edition should give its reader something complete. Neither identical cross-posting nor zero overlap is required. No need to be everywhere, publish at someone else's frequency or append a sales CTA to every edition. Revisit the choices from actual response and sustainable effort.

Platform context: [YouTube on multiple formats](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-creators-full-time-business-tools/) and [LinkedIn on sharing professional expertise](https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/content-marketing/creating-a-thought-leadership-marketing-plan). These support the available approaches, not a guaranteed outcome or an algorithm prescription. Checked 20 November 2026.

## Make only the choices that change the work

| Area | Record the actual choice |
| --- | --- |
| Starting material | Written work, recordings, spoken notes, existing content, analog notes or a combination |
| Creative process | What the human wants to do; what AI may help with; outline versus full script; human editor or existing workflow |
| Formats | A small useful set such as reflection/article, guide, how-to, showcase or direct short note |
| Channels | Audience, language, useful format, contribution to the goal and sustainable effort for each active destination |
| Editorial original | Where each kind of text/production is actually edited; Notion can index an existing external original |
| Media/projects | Owner-selected storage, access, revision and backup responsibility; chosen editor and qualification status |
| Delivery | Actual account/destination, draft/review path, applicable standing or task authority and where outcomes are recorded |
| Learning | Relevant signals, observation window, sustainable effort and the next review/experiment |

Notion for editorial text and Kit for newsletter delivery are a starter recipe; suitable existing tools can stay. HyperFrames is a candidate requiring a real import/edit/save-reopen/export qualification before use is claimed. None of these choices installs a tool or connects an account.

A newsletter may be an original or a synthesis of several productions. Video, email, an article and a social post can each be long or short. Long-to-short reuse is useful when each edition has a self-contained point; do not force every idea into a long production or every production onto every channel.

## Keep creative references useful

Use the [reference-library workflow](reference-library.md) when a creator example should inform the owner's style. Keep the original and observations on its Source, accepted choices in configuration, and reusable techniques in the existing style or treatment guide. Update a personal skill only when its working steps change. Each production selects the references it needs; a saved source is not automatic adoption. Channel languages are owner choices, not shared defaults.

## Decide participation per responsibility

For writing style, use the [writing-voice workflow](writing-voice.md): select genuine examples, distinguish minimal editing from new drafting, and learn from exact owner corrections. The concise profile stays with its existing Knowledge owner and permitted originals or pointers in Content. Use existing metadata and relevant language/channel examples, not another database or mandatory extra skill. Rejected candidates stay rejected; publication and engagement are not voice approval. Calibration can remain partial.

| Responsibility | Useful configuration |
| --- | --- |
| Setup | Existing routes, reusable schema, missing structure and acceptance example |
| Intake | Capture sources and useful angles; decide whether scouting is wanted |
| Prepare | Writing/recording support, required evidence and material handoff |
| Produce / Distribute | Selected editor, editions, review and actual delivery policy |
| Learn | Goal-relevant observations and deliberate improvement |

Each can be manual, assisted or selectively automated. A responsibility is not a mandatory separate skill or agent. Keep a good analog workflow. Missing input blocks only the dependent operation; an idea can still be captured while media editing is unavailable.

For an automation, specify the real trigger/cadence and timezone, selected sources/account, bounded work, output, permitted actions, missing-input behavior, retry/reconciliation and native job identity. Create it only under applicable authority. Use the same adopted method as a manual invocation; no second implementation or background publisher hidden in a skill.

## Adopt personal skills

The five public templates are starting material. Read and adapt the useful ones through the owner's available Manage Skills method. Do not require an unreleased OSM name: discover the actually installed capability or follow the selected home's supported personal-Skills procedure.

Before writing, query the owner's Skills for semantic overlap and preserve useful existing instructions, supporting files and provenance. Choose discriminating names/descriptions, actual input/output locations and a reference to the accepted owner configuration. Shared methods stay outside that configuration. A bundle copied locally is not a native install or automatic enablement.

The setup template uses the schema/setup contract. Other templates need only relevant goals, configuration and selected material. They do not re-interview the owner or recreate the database at every request. If the configuration is missing, resolve the actual gap rather than guess accounts, destinations or permission.

## A compact owner configuration

Store the concrete choices in one short private Knowledge record using the owner's normal schema. Its body can contain this simple record, completed from evidence; these are descriptive fields, not an executable deployment format:

```yaml
content_workflow:
  context_home: null
  definition_binding_ref: null
  editorial_fields_and_views: {}
  key_convention: null
  integration_triggers_and_safe_states: []
  goals_and_revisions: []
  audience_and_voice_refs: []
  editorial_originals: []
  media_and_backup_routes: []
  selected_channels: []
  formats_and_preferences: []
  stage_ownership_and_modes: {}
  tool_routes_and_qualification: {}
  delivery_policy: null
  observation_and_review: null
  adopted_personal_skills: []
  actual_automation_ids: []
```

The binding reference points to the existing Definition or source agreement; its verified route and minimum fields are not copied here. The editorial map contains only selected workflow additions and views. Trigger and safe-state notes refer to the authoritative source agreement rather than competing with it.

Null/empty means unresolved or not selected, not a working default. Do not activate missing tools, delivery or schedules to fill this record. Capture the concrete blocker and continue independent useful work. Update configuration only when a supported choice changes; routine production belongs in Content.

Each media route names its location, host where relevant, actual access and backup owner/status. Each adopted skill records its real personal-Skills location/ID, source template/version/hash, local adaptations and observed discovery/invocation state. Capture actual evidence; do not label a fetched page as enabled discovery. If neither skill management nor the home's supported Skills operation is available, report the exact gap and retain the proposed adaptation without creating an improvised second store.
