# Learn the author's writing voice

A useful voice profile helps an assistant preserve how a person thinks and
writes. It is sometimes called voice DNA. Here it means selected examples,
explicit preferences and learning from corrections, not model training or
an imitation of another creator.

Use the existing writing method and owner configuration. This adds a way to
calibrate them; it creates no extra skill, database, or publishing job.
The [voice profile](../templates/knowledge-entries/voice-profile.md) is the body
shape for the existing Knowledge voice record. Measure it from the owner's own
posts. Channel skills link to that record. The [setup prompts](../templates/knowledge-entries/setup-interview.md)
resolve only missing input. Keep measured traits distinct from explicit owner
preferences, and report partial calibration honestly.
A short writing request can be completed without calibrating a whole profile.

## Select evidence, not an entire archive

Start with a small, deliberately selected set of the author's actual writing
and relevant speech. Read only what helps this language, channel and situation.
Keep original date, location, authorship and permission to retain it. A dictated
instruction to an assistant is not automatically an example of public prose.
Third-party references can inform presentation, but cannot establish this
author's voice. A published text may have an editor or AI co-author.

Reuse the existing homes:

| Evidence | Where it belongs |
| --- | --- |
| Concise accepted preferences, scope and example links | The existing voice/style record in Knowledge |
| Permitted original writing or a pointer to it | The existing Content Source, preserving provenance |
| Draft, candidate, owner correction and final edition | Their existing production or Edition, with version relationships |
| Supported change to the working procedure | The existing personal method, when its steps actually change |

Use existing fields or a short body section for language, channel, situation,
date, authorship and approval; do not add a column for each. In the optional
starter metadata or body notes, `Kind = Own writing` describes authorship and `Review = Voice approved`
means the owner accepted it as representative. These are separate decisions.
Record AI assistance, editing or transcription explicitly; accepting a delivered
Edition does not turn mixed authorship into solely author-written evidence.
Do not label an AI draft as `Own writing`. An owner may explicitly identify
specific assisted wording as representative, but keep that provenance visible
and keep genuine author-written examples in the calibration set; never let
approved AI outputs silently replace them.
Source-only examples stay in their original system; an accessible link is not
permission to copy the text into a profile, review brief or archive.

If examples are thin, use the current guide and supplied draft, state the
limited calibration and continue the requested writing. Do not manufacture a
persona or require fifty posts before helping.

## Choose the editing task

For an existing draft, make the smallest change that meets the request.
Shortening first removes redundancy while preserving thought order, useful
word choices, punctuation and readable conversational grammar. Keep uncertainty
such as "I think" when it is part of the meaning. A shorter polished slogan is
not a faithful edit merely because it mentions the same topics. Fixed approved
wording remains fixed; flag a conflict with a length limit rather than silently
rewriting it.

For a new draft, read a few relevant accepted examples and the current voice
guide. Follow the manner without borrowing unrelated opinions, experiences or
claims. A Danish personal reflection and an English professional reply may
need different examples; one correction does not define all languages and
channels. The current request can deliberately ask for a different treatment.

For calibration, compare the original, candidate and owner's actual correction.
Distinguish an observed pattern from a hypothesis. Ask a focused question only
when the difference matters and cannot be resolved from existing evidence.

## Learn from the correction

Keep this small chain on the work that produced it:

1. Original and the requested change.
2. Candidate and its exact version.
3. Owner correction or accepted final, with the scope of that acceptance.
4. One supported preference, or a clearly labelled hypothesis linked to this
   Edition for a relevant future comparison.

Retain rejected candidates as rejected, including why they failed. They are not
positive voice examples. Publication, engagement, or accepting one sentence
does not approve the whole text as representative. Accepting a final Edition
for delivery is not approval of a standing rule or the whole voice profile.
Do not turn one rejected
phrase into a universal banned word, or add deliberate mistakes to sound human.
An unchanged draft or no new preference is a valid result.

A direct owner instruction about future writing is already evidence for the
scoped preference; do not ask again merely to save it under existing authority.
A diagnosis of why one candidate failed belongs on that candidate; it is not
a standing voice rule by itself. Keep factual accuracy as a meaning check,
rather than inventing a channel-specific voice preference from a factual error.
For an inferred pattern, propose the rule and its scope before treating it as
accepted guidance. Keep an unconfirmed hypothesis on its Edition or Source, with a link
from the relevant voice record's clearly separate pending observations. On a
relevant later task, test it only when requested or useful to the current brief;
then confirm it from the owner's correction or retire it. It is never an accepted
rule merely because it was retrieved.

Update the existing voice record or personal method within the selected
maintenance authority. Keep the evidence and narrower exceptions. A newer
explicit preference supersedes the conflicting older rule in that scope; mark
the earlier rule or example as superseded or no longer representative, preserving
its history. Repeating an already recorded correction creates no duplicate
lesson. A changing production status stays on its Edition, not in Memory or the
voice profile.

## Review the exact final text

Check these independently:

- Meaning: facts, qualifications, commitments, authorship and thought order.
- Voice: wording, pace, sentence structure and grammar for this situation.
- Destination: the chosen format, exact length and required links or credit.

Count the actual final version, not a previous candidate. Use the destination's
native counter when available; its treatment of links and characters can differ
from a local count. If only a local check is possible, report its method and
limit rather than claiming native validation. Keep an oversize draft open for
editing. Review success never grants permission to publish.
